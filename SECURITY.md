# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security vulnerability in Griffin31 or in a repository of this organization, email **[support@griffin31.ai](mailto:support@griffin31.ai?subject=Security%20issue%20report)** with the subject "Security issue report".

Please include:

- what is affected (the product area or repository, and its version or commit)
- the steps to reproduce it
- the impact you expect

Please don't open a public issue or share the details publicly until we have fixed it. We'll confirm we received your report and keep you updated until it's resolved.

## More

Our security and compliance practices are described in the [Trust Center](https://kb.griffin31.com/trust-center/).
